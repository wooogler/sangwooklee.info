# Bridging people with AI agents.

I'm **Sangwook Lee**, a third-year Ph.D. candidate in [Computer Science at Virginia Tech](https://cs.vt.edu), advised by [Prof. Sang Won Lee](https://echolab.cs.vt.edu/sangwonlee/) in the [echolab](https://echolab.cs.vt.edu/). I work at the intersection of **Human-AI Interaction**, **social computing**, and **accessibility**.

I build and deploy **LLM-based agents that bridge gaps between people**, while keeping users in control of what agents do on their behalf:

- **Knowledge gap**: turning everyday lab communication into organizational memory ([CHOIR](/publications/choir), CHI '26 Honorable Mention)
- **Digital divide**: adapting GUIs and navigation to each user's preferences ([MAESTRO](/publications/maestro), UIST '26)
- **Expertise gap**: letting domain experts, not engineers, shape AI behavior ([ModSandbox](/publications/modsandbox), CHI '23; SCORE, in progress)
