# Bridging people with AI agents.

I'm **Sangwook Lee**, a third-year Ph.D. candidate at [Virginia Tech Computer Science](https://cs.vt.edu), advised by [Prof. Sang Won Lee](https://echolab.cs.vt.edu/sangwonlee/) in the [echolab](https://echolab.cs.vt.edu/). My research sits at the intersection of **Human-AI Interaction (HAI)**, **social computing**, and **accessibility**.

I design, build, and deploy **LLM-based agents that bridge gaps between people**, while keeping end users in control of what agents do on their behalf. My Ph.D. research unfolds across three threads:

- **Bridging the knowledge gap**: In collaborative organizations (e.g., university research labs), a *knowledge agent* turns everyday communication into organizational memory, reducing information asymmetry among members ([CHOIR](/publications/choir), CHI '26 Best Paper Honorable Mention).
- **Bridging the digital divide**: In complex digital systems (e.g., movie ticket booking), a *UI agent* adapts the interface and navigation to each user's preferences, reducing accessibility gaps across user populations ([MAESTRO](/publications/maestro), UIST '26).
- **Keeping humans in control**: In classrooms, instructors configure a course chatbot as a set of intents and trace every response back to the instruction that produced it, so they can see and fix how the chatbot behaves (SCORE, in progress).
