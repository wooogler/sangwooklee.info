\cvsection{Research Interests}
I design, build, and deploy LLM-based agents that \textbf{bridge gaps between people}, spanning human-AI interaction, social computing, and accessibility, while keeping end users in control of what agents do on their behalf:
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.3em,itemsep=1.5pt,topsep=3pt]\raggedright
  \item \textbf{Knowledge gap} — turning everyday lab communication into organizational memory (CHOIR, CHI~'26)
  \item \textbf{Digital divide} — adapting GUIs and navigation to each user's preferences (MAESTRO, UIST~'26)
  \item \textbf{Human control} — letting instructors configure and audit classroom chatbots (SCORE)
\end{itemize}
